📖 How to Play

Get gold → claim a Character → maybe lie → someone can challenge → being wrong costs you a Character.

Objective

Golden Bluff is for 2–6 players. Be the first to collect 10 Golden Tickets — or the last player left holding any Character cards at all.

Setup

Each player draws 2 secret Character cards and starts with 0 tickets. Nobody else can see your cards — but you can claim to have any Character in the game, whether you actually do or not.

Your Turn

1. Stable Income — take +1 Golden Ticket automatically. This can't be challenged, and if it brings you to 10, you win instantly.

2. Make a Claim — announce any Character (except Guard — it's never claimed on your own turn) and its ability, targeting another player if the ability needs one. You may or may not actually have that Character.

3. Challenge Window — everyone else gets a chance to call CHALLENGE. If nobody does, your claim happens, true or not.

Getting Challenged

If you were bluffing: your claim is cancelled, the challenger gains 1 ticket, and you choose one of your own Character cards to discard.

If you were telling the truth: you gain 1 ticket for winning the challenge, the challenger discards one of their cards, and your claim still happens. Your revealed card then gets shuffled back into the deck and you draw a fresh secret replacement, so nobody knows what you're holding now.

Characters = Your Lives

Your 2 Character cards are also your health. Discard one and you're down to your last life; discard your final card and you're eliminated — your tickets return to the bank.

The Royal Counter-Claim

When someone claims Royal, instead of challenging you can claim "I'm also a Royal!" — a counter-claim that goes through the exact same challenge process. If your counter holds up, the original claimant's payout is capped at 1 ticket instead of 2.

Guard — the reactive block

Guard is never claimed on your own turn. If you're targeted by a Thief, Seer, or Assassin claim, you may reveal an actual Guard card the instant it's about to happen, blocking it outright. No Guard card in hand, no block.

Characters

Winning

First to 10+ Golden Tickets wins. Reaching 10 through Stable Income wins instantly and can't be stopped — reaching 10 through a claimed ability still has to survive the challenge window first.
